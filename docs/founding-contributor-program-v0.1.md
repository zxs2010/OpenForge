# OpenForge Founding Contributor Program v0.1

**Status:** Active  
**Program start:** 2026-08-31  
**Languages:** [中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

### 为什么设立这个计划

OpenForge 希望由真实需求、开发者、创作者、AI 项目、工具、算力与渠道共同建设。早期贡献不能只换来一句“感谢”，也不能被模糊的未来收益承诺所替代。

Founding Contributor 计划用四种清楚、可验证的方式认可早期建设者：

1. 永久公开的贡献记录；
2. 从 Contributor 到 Reviewer、Maintainer 的治理成长路径；
3. 仅在资金已经确认时发布的任务赏金；
4. 参与真实 Activity 时，在开工前单独约定的项目报酬。

Founding Contributor 是对真实早期贡献的荣誉身份，不是股权、Token、投资产品、雇佣或合伙关系，也不保证未来订单、赏金、收入、平台分红或治理席位。

### 计划窗口

计划从 2026 年 8 月 31 日开始。结束时间不早于 2026 年 11 月 29 日，并且不早于 OpenForge 首个可重复运行的真实生产闭环公开后 30 天。维护者会至少提前 30 天在 Discussions 公告最终截止日期。

计划不采用“前 N 名”抢位。窗口内任何达到公开标准的人都可以申请。

### 哪些贡献符合资格

申请人应遵守 [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)，并至少完成一项被合并、接受或公开采纳的实质成果，例如：

- 可运行的 Provider、Agent、Skill Adapter 或协议一致性测试；
- Todo Bridge、Activity、Job、人工审核或交付闭环中的可验证模块；
- 真实的安全、隐私、可靠性、可访问性或国际化改进；
- 被采纳的协议提案、RFC、生产工作流、文档、设计或示例；
- 有复现证据的关键问题报告、测试方案或发布验证；
- 促成并记录一个真实的人、AI、项目、工具、算力或渠道节点连接；
- 持续且高质量的 Issue 分类、代码评审、导师或社区维护工作。

纯格式修改、重复 Issue、未验证的批量 AI 内容、只 Fork/Star/转发、未被接受的宣传，以及人为拆分的小提交，不单独构成资格。

### Founding Contributor 获得什么

- 在版本控制的 [Founding Contributors Ledger](../FOUNDING_CONTRIBUTORS.md) 中永久记录公开身份、贡献类型、日期与证据链接；
- 使用 `OpenForge Founding Contributor · 2026` 公开身份，并链接到可验证记录；
- 在相关 Release Notes、演示和公开案例中按实际贡献署名；贡献者可以选择匿名或使用公开化名；
- 进入 Contributor → Reviewer → Maintainer 的公开成长路径；
- 参与公开 RFC、路线图讨论和对应工作组；
- 当公开能力与真实 Activity 匹配时，获得项目邀请与完整公开说明的机会；邀请不等于中选或收入保证；
- 在符合条件时，展示个人或组织的公开赞助链接。

### 治理成长路径

| 角色 | 基本条件 | 权限与责任 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant | 参与讨论、使用或反馈 | 提案、问答并遵守社区规则 |
| Contributor | 至少一项被接受的实质成果 | 进入贡献账本，维护自己的贡献 |
| Reviewer | 在一个领域持续贡献并完成可靠评审 | 对对应领域 PR/RFC 正式评审，不能独自批准自己的改动 |
| Maintainer | 长期可靠并理解兼容性、安全和社区边界 | 合并、发布和维护对应领域，并培养新的维护者 |

Founding Contributor 是永久荣誉标签，但不会跳过 Reviewer 或 Maintainer 的晋升标准。高风险权限始终与持续信任和责任绑定。

### 赏金与真实项目报酬

没有确认资金的任务只能标记为 `help wanted` 或 `priority-task`，不能称为赏金。

只有带有 `funded-bounty` 标签并在开工前公开以下信息的任务，才属于现金赏金：

- 金额与法币币种；
- 付款主体与支付方式；
- 范围、交付物与验收标准；
- 认领方式、时间和取消条件；
- 多人协作时的金额分配；
- 许可证、知识产权和必要的保密边界；
- 争议与申诉方式。

真实客户或社区 Activity 的报酬由参与者在开工前单独确认。每个 Activity 独立约定预算、角色、交付、验收、知识产权和付款条件，不形成对平台未来收入的永久权利。

### 申请与评审

1. 使用 **Founding Contributor application** Issue 模板提交申请；
2. 列出贡献类别以及 PR、Issue、Discussion、Activity、Artifact 或其他公开证据；
3. 说明你对成果的责任，以及 AI 或第三方工具的使用方式；
4. 维护者检查资格、许可证、安全和利益冲突；
5. 申请公开接受社区反馈；通过后更新贡献账本。

在第二位合格 Reviewer 加入前，申请由当前维护者评审，并保留不少于 7 天的公开反馈期。之后，申请不得只由申请人本人或单一利益相关者批准。

安全贡献不得为了申请身份而公开漏洞。请先通过 GitHub 的私密安全报告渠道联系维护者；问题修复后，可以使用去敏记录作为贡献证据。

### 公平与反作弊

- 按被接受、可维护的成果评审，不按 commit、Issue、评论、Star 或邀请人数计分；
- 重复、拆分、换账号提交和互相刷认可的内容合并评估；
- 允许使用 AI，但申请人必须披露并对正确性、许可证、隐私和安全负责；
- 付费成果必须由非作者 Reviewer 验收；利益相关者应公开披露并回避最终决定；
- 欺诈、冒名、伪造证据或严重违反行为准则可以导致身份更正或移除；
- Reviewer/Maintainer 权限可因长期不活跃、安全风险或违规暂停；真实、无争议的历史贡献记录原则上保留。

### 开源、独立性与保密

提交到 OpenForge 仓库并被接受的贡献按照 [Apache License 2.0](../LICENSE) 发布。独立的 AI、项目、Provider、Skill、Agent、算力或渠道节点继续保留自己的仓库、许可证、知识产权与商业模式。

申请和公开讨论不得包含 API 密钥、个人信息、客户素材、私有仓库内容、未授权代码、内部 Prompt、合同、报价、未公开漏洞或其他商业机密。只能提交本人有权公开的证据。无法确认的信息应先私下询问维护者。

### 计划变更

v0.1 是早期制度。任何重要变更都会在 Discussions 公开提案，说明原因、生效日期以及对已有贡献者的影响。已经被真实接受的贡献不会因为规则更新而被悄悄抹除。

## English

### Purpose

OpenForge is being built by real needs, developers, creators, AI projects, tools, compute, and distribution channels. Early work deserves more than a thank-you, but it must not be exchanged for vague promises of future wealth.

The Founding Contributor Program recognizes early builders through four separate tracks:

1. permanent, public, verifiable contribution records;
2. a documented path from Contributor to Reviewer and Maintainer;
3. cash bounties only when funding is confirmed in advance; and
4. separately agreed compensation for real Activities.

Founding Contributor is an honorary record of real early work. It is not equity, a Token, an investment product, employment, or a partnership. It does not guarantee future work, bounties, income, platform revenue, or a governance seat.

### Program window

The program begins on August 31, 2026. It will close no earlier than November 29, 2026, and no earlier than 30 days after OpenForge publicly demonstrates its first repeatable real-production loop. Maintainers will announce the final closing date in Discussions at least 30 days in advance.

This is not a first-N race. Anyone who meets the published standard during the window may apply.

### Qualifying contributions

Applicants must follow the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and complete at least one substantial contribution that is merged, accepted, or publicly adopted. Examples include:

- a working Provider, Agent, or Skill adapter, or a protocol conformance test;
- a verifiable module in the Todo Bridge, Activity, Job, human-review, or delivery loop;
- a real security, privacy, reliability, accessibility, or internationalization improvement;
- an adopted protocol proposal, RFC, production workflow, document, design, or example;
- a reproducible high-impact issue report, test plan, or release verification;
- a documented connection of a real person, AI, project, tool, compute, or channel node;
- sustained, high-quality issue triage, review, mentoring, or community maintenance.

Formatting-only changes, duplicate issues, unverified bulk AI output, forks, stars, reposts, unaccepted promotion, and artificially split micro-contributions do not qualify on their own.

### What contributors receive

- a permanent entry in the version-controlled [Founding Contributors Ledger](../FOUNDING_CONTRIBUTORS.md), linked to public evidence;
- permission to use the `OpenForge Founding Contributor · 2026` identity with a link to that record;
- accurate attribution in relevant release notes, demos, and public case studies, with an option to remain anonymous or use a public pseudonym;
- entry into the documented Contributor → Reviewer → Maintainer path;
- participation in public RFCs, roadmap discussions, and relevant working groups;
- the opportunity to receive a project invitation when declared capabilities match a real Activity, without any guarantee of selection or income;
- visibility for a public sponsorship link when eligible.

### Governance path

| Role | Baseline | Authority and responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant | Discusses, uses, or reports | Proposes and asks questions while following community rules |
| Contributor | Has at least one accepted substantial outcome | Appears in the ledger and maintains their contribution |
| Reviewer | Contributes continuously in one area and completes reliable reviews | Formally reviews related PRs/RFCs and cannot approve their own work alone |
| Maintainer | Demonstrates sustained reliability and understands compatibility, security, and community boundaries | Merges, releases, maintains a domain, and develops future maintainers |

Founding Contributor is a permanent recognition label, not a shortcut around Reviewer or Maintainer criteria. High-risk permissions remain tied to continuing trust and responsibility.

### Bounties and paid Activities

Unfunded tasks may use `help wanted` or `priority-task`; they must not be called bounties.

Only an issue labeled `funded-bounty` and documenting the following before work starts is a cash bounty:

- amount and fiat currency;
- payer and payment method;
- scope, deliverables, and acceptance criteria;
- claim process, timing, and cancellation terms;
- allocation for collaborative work;
- license, intellectual property, and necessary confidentiality terms;
- dispute and appeal process.

Compensation for a real customer or community Activity is confirmed separately before work begins. Each Activity defines its own budget, roles, deliverables, acceptance, intellectual property, and payment terms. It creates no permanent claim on future platform revenue.

### Application and review

1. Open the **Founding Contributor application** issue template.
2. Identify the contribution type and link PR, Issue, Discussion, Activity, Artifact, or other public evidence.
3. State your responsibility for the result and disclose meaningful AI or third-party tool use.
4. Maintainers review eligibility, licensing, security, and conflicts of interest.
5. The application remains open for community feedback; an accepted application is added to the ledger.

Until a second qualified Reviewer joins, the current maintainer reviews applications with a public feedback period of at least seven days. Afterwards, no applicant or single interested party may approve an application alone.

Do not disclose a vulnerability to obtain recognition. Report it privately through GitHub security reporting first. A sanitized record may be used as evidence after remediation.

### Fairness and anti-abuse

- evaluate accepted, maintainable outcomes rather than commit, issue, comment, star, or referral counts;
- assess duplicates, artificial splitting, alternate accounts, and reciprocal recognition as one body of work;
- permit AI assistance only when the applicant discloses it and remains accountable for correctness, licensing, privacy, and security;
- require a non-author Reviewer for paid acceptance and conflict disclosure or recusal;
- correct or remove recognition obtained through fraud, impersonation, falsified evidence, or serious Code of Conduct violations;
- allow Reviewer/Maintainer access to be paused for inactivity, security risk, or misconduct while retaining legitimate historical contribution records.

### Open source, independence, and confidentiality

Accepted contributions submitted to the OpenForge repository are released under the [Apache License 2.0](../LICENSE). Independent AI systems, projects, Providers, Skills, Agents, compute services, and channels retain their own repositories, licenses, intellectual property, and business models.

Applications and public discussions must not contain API keys, personal information, customer assets, private-repository content, unauthorized code, internal prompts, contracts, pricing, undisclosed vulnerabilities, or other confidential information. Submit only evidence you are authorized to make public. Ask a maintainer privately when unsure.

### Changes to the program

Version 0.1 is an early policy. Material changes will be proposed in Discussions with a reason, effective date, and impact statement. Legitimate accepted contributions will not be silently erased by later rule changes.
