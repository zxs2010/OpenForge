# OpenForge Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in OpenForge respectful, safe, and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
ability, nationality, appearance, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and make room for people with less context.
- Credit ideas and work accurately.
- Respect privacy, consent, and reasonable boundaries.
- Focus disagreement on proposals and evidence, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, or retaliation are not acceptable. Publishing
another person's private information without permission is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other
spaces where someone is representing OpenForge.

## Reporting and enforcement

Until a private reporting address is published, report an incident privately
to a repository owner through their GitHub profile rather than opening a public
issue. Repository owners will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably
possible, review reports promptly and fairly, and may remove content, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.

No one should investigate or decide a report in which they are directly
involved. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is itself a violation.

This document will be updated with named enforcement contacts as the maintainer
team forms.
