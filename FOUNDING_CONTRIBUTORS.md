## Record policy

- Each entry links to an accepted PR, Issue, Discussion, Activity, Artifact, or another public record.
- Recognition follows the published program and community review process.
- Legitimate historical records remain even when an active Reviewer or Maintainer role later changes.
- Fraudulent, impersonated, or falsified records may be corrected or removed with a public reason.
